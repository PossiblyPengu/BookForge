A new space. Alien but reminiscent of something freshly uncovered, but already slipping away from my conscious being. A place broken out from inside me, into the world myself and the rest of the living had tacitly accepted as being true.

I crawled to my knees. The room was dark, except for pale dawn light slipping through holes in a coned ceiling. Dark and large and round. A small dead tree planted in a large metal pot in the center of the room. An old beaten up saucepan and other objects scattered out before it.
